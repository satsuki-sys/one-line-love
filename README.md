An entry for the make.fun bounty **Make the Judge Fall in Love in One Line**: one line, 240 characters at most, that tries to make the reader fall in love with a person. The program (`main.py`, stdlib only, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> She dog-ears every library book at the page where she stops believing in the ending, so the next stranger knows exactly where to give up too.
